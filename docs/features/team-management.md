# Team & Employee Management

This document explains how companies organize their sales staff into teams in simple words.

---

## 1. Overview

Admins can structure their sales reps into teams (like "Inbound Reps" or "Mid-Market Sales"). Each team has an assigned Manager who oversees the team's workload and leads.

---

## 2. Managing Employees

Admins can:
- **Add New Employees:** Provide their name, email, and role (`Admin`, `Manager`, or `Employee`).
- **Temporary Password:** New accounts receive a temporary password and are prompted to change it on first login.
- **Change Roles:** Promote or change user roles safely.
- **Deactivate Accounts:** When an employee leaves, their account can be deactivated immediately. This revokes their login sessions so they cannot access company data.
- **Reactivate Accounts:** Easily restore access if an employee returns.

---

## 3. Managing Teams

Admins can:
- Create new teams with a name and description.
- Pick a Manager to lead each team.
- Add sales reps to a team.
- Remove sales reps from a team.

### Important Rule: One Active Team per Employee
An employee can only belong to one active sales team at a time. The database automatically prevents assigning someone to two teams at once.

---

## 4. Manager Access Boundaries

- A **Manager** can only view and manage their own team's leads, tasks, and members.
- A Manager cannot view, reassign, or alter leads belonging to another Manager's team.
- Managers cannot create new user accounts or change user roles.
