# Follow-ups & Task Scheduling

This document explains how follow-ups, calls, and task reminders work in simple words.

---

## 1. Why Follow-ups Matter

Sales deals are often lost simply because reps forget to follow up. The Follow-ups module helps sales teams:
- Schedule calls, emails, meetings, and tasks for any lead.
- Keep track of what needs to be done today.
- Automatically see red badges for overdue tasks.
- Keep a clean history of every completed touchpoint.

---

## 2. Task Details

When you create a follow-up, you choose:
- **Lead:** Which customer or deal this task belongs to.
- **Type:**
  - `CALL`: Phone call.
  - `EMAIL`: Email outreach.
  - `MEETING`: Video call, demo, or in-person meeting.
  - `TASK`: General to-do item (contract review, research).
- **Priority:** `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, or `HIGH`.
- **Due Date & Time:** When the task should be finished.
- **Assigned To:** The sales rep responsible for doing it.

---

## 3. Task Statuses

- **Pending:** The task is scheduled and waiting to be done.
- **Due Today:** Automatically highlighted when the due date is today.
- **Overdue:** Automatically highlighted in red when the due date has passed.
- **Completed:** When finished, the user enters outcome notes (e.g., "Demo went great, sending proposal tomorrow").
- **Cancelled:** Marked if the task is no longer needed.

Once a task is marked `Completed` or `Cancelled`, it is locked and cannot be edited.

---

## 4. Who Can See What

- **Admins:** Can see all follow-ups across the entire company.
- **Managers:** Can see all follow-ups for their own team members.
- **Employees:** Can see only their own assigned follow-up tasks.
