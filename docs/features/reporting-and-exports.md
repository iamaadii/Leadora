# Reports & Data Exports

This document explains how users preview and export reports in simple words.

---

## 1. Available Reports

Users can generate reports for:
- **Leads:** All leads with contact details, company, source, stage, value, and assigned rep.
- **Conversions:** Deals that reached `Won` status, with win dates and revenue values.
- **Lost Leads:** Deals that were lost, including the recorded reason for loss.
- **Follow-ups:** Scheduled tasks, completion dates, and outcomes.
- **Employee Directory:** Staff members, roles, teams, and active lead counts.
- **Team Performance:** Team workload, stage breakdown, and quota progress.
- **Activity Feed:** Audit logs of calls, emails, and stage movements.

---

## 2. 1-Click File Downloads

You can download any report in three different formats with one click:

1. **CSV (.csv):**
   - Pure plain-text table data.
   - Ideal for importing into spreadsheets or external business tools.
   - Includes formula injection protection (prevents dangerous spreadsheet formulas like `=cmd`).

2. **Excel (.xlsx):**
   - Formatted spreadsheet with a professional look.
   - Styled dark blue header row.
   - Auto-fitted column widths and currency formatting.

3. **PDF (.pdf):**
   - Clean, professional document layout.
   - Summary statistics cards at the top.
   - Numbered pages and formatted data tables ready to share with executives or clients.

---

## 3. Fast Streaming

Reports stream directly from the server to your browser. You don't have to wait for background email links or polling queues.
