# Organization Settings

This document explains company settings in simple words.

---

## 1. Overview

Each company workspace has its own settings page where Admins can customize their company profile.

---

## 2. Settings Available

- **Company Name:** The name of your business displayed in headers and on exported PDF reports.
- **Currency Code & Symbol:**
  - `USD ($)`: US Dollars
  - `EUR (€)`: Euros
  - `GBP (£)`: British Pounds
  - `INR (₹)`: Indian Rupees
  - `CAD ($)`: Canadian Dollars
  - `AUD ($)`: Australian Dollars
- **Timezone:** Local timezone (e.g. `America/New_York`, `Europe/London`, `Asia/Kolkata`). This ensures all follow-up due dates and activity timestamps display in your local time.

---

## 3. Permissions

- Only users with the **Admin** role can change company settings.
- Managers and Employees can view the settings, but cannot edit them.
