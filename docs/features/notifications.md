# In-App Notifications

This document explains the notification bell and user alerts in simple words.

---

## 1. What are Notifications?

The notification center is a bell icon located in the top navigation bar. It alerts sales reps and managers about important events without requiring email or SMS.

---

## 2. When are Notifications Sent?

- **Lead Assignment:** When an Admin or Manager assigns a lead to you, an instant alert is created:
  *"New lead assigned: Sarah Jenkins (Acme Corp)"*.
- **Due Today Reminder:** When a scheduled follow-up is due today, you receive a morning alert reminding you of the call or meeting.

---

## 3. How Users Interact with Alerts

- **Unread Badge:** A colored badge on the bell icon shows the exact number of unread alerts.
- **Mark as Read:** Clicking a notification marks it as read.
- **Mark All Read:** A single button marks the entire feed as read.
