# Public Live Demo Environment

This document explains how the free interactive demo works in simple words.

---

## 1. What is the Live Demo?

The Live Demo (`/demo`) lets prospective customers test the Leadora CRM instantly without signing up, entering a credit card, or logging in.

---

## 2. What Can Users Do in the Demo?

- **Kanban Board:** View sample leads and drag cards across the 7 pipeline stages.
- **Follow-ups:** Inspect scheduled calls, meetings, and overdue alerts.
- **Analytics Dashboard:** Explore sample charts, revenue win rates, and pipeline funnels.
- **Role Switcher:** Switch between Admin, Manager, and Rep perspectives to see how menus and data boundaries change.
- **Reports:** Preview formatted sales and lead reports.

---

## 3. Demo Safety

- The demo uses a dedicated sample workspace.
- It is completely isolated from real customer accounts.
- Changes made in the demo do not affect real company data.
