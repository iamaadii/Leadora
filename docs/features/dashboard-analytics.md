# Dashboard & Sales Analytics

This document explains the Leadora dashboard and reports analytics in simple words.

---

## 1. Executive Summary Cards

At the top of the dashboard, users see their most important metrics:
- **Total Pipeline Value:** Total dollar value of all active deals.
- **Active Leads:** How many prospective customers are currently being worked on.
- **Win Rate:** Percentage of closed deals that were won (`Won / (Won + Lost)`).
- **Follow-ups Due Today:** Number of calls, demos, or tasks scheduled for today.

---

## 2. Interactive Charts & Funnels

- **Pipeline Funnel:** Shows how many deals and how much value are currently in each of the 7 stages (`New` → `Contacted` → `Qualified` → `Proposal Sent` → `Negotiation` → `Won` → `Lost`).
- **Lead Trends:** A chart showing how many new leads were created over time (by day, week, or month).
- **Task Analytics:** Shows how many follow-ups were completed on time versus overdue.

---

## 3. Team & Rep Leaderboard (Admins & Managers)

Admins and Managers can review:
- Each sales rep's active leads.
- Total won deal value per rep.
- Average days to close a deal.
- Workload balance to make sure no single rep is overloaded with too many leads.
