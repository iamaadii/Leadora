# Leads & Kanban Board

This document explains the Leadora visual sales board in simple words.

---

## 1. What is the Kanban Board?

The Kanban board lets sales teams view all active leads as visual cards arranged into 7 columns. Each column represents a stage in the sales process.

You can move cards between columns by:
- Dragging and dropping them with your mouse.
- Selecting a new stage from the dropdown inside each card (for mobile or keyboard users).

---

## 2. The 7 Sales Stages

1. **New:** Brand new leads that have not been contacted yet.
2. **Contacted:** The sales rep made the first call or sent the first email.
3. **Qualified:** The customer has verified interest, need, and budget.
4. **Proposal Sent:** A price quote, demo, or formal proposal was given.
5. **Negotiation:** The customer is discussing price, terms, or scope.
6. **Won:** The deal is closed! The customer agreed and bought.
7. **Lost:** The deal did not go through. A short reason is saved.

---

## 3. What Each Card Shows

Each card on the board shows:
- **Lead Name:** Click to open the full lead details page (`/leads/:id`).
- **Company Name:** The company the lead works for.
- **Estimated Value:** How much money the deal is worth (e.g. `$25,000`).
- **Lead Source:** Badge showing where the lead came from (Website, Referral, Event).
- **Assigned Rep:** The avatar and name of the sales rep in charge.

---

## 4. Switching Views

At the top of the Leads screen, you can easily switch between:
- **List View (`/leads`):** A spreadsheet-style table with sortable columns and batch filters.
- **Kanban View (`/leads/kanban`):** The interactive drag-and-drop board.
