# Activity History & Audit Feed

This document explains how Leadora tracks customer interactions and system changes in simple words.

---

## 1. What is the Activity History?

Every lead has a chronological timeline showing everything that happened to it. This ensures that anyone opening a lead can immediately see what has already been done and what happened in past conversations.

---

## 2. Automatic Events

The system automatically writes an activity log entry whenever:
- A new lead is created.
- The lead's sales stage changes (e.g. from `New` to `Contacted`).
- The lead is assigned or reassigned to a different sales rep.
- A scheduled follow-up is created, completed, or cancelled.
- A lead is archived or restored.

---

## 3. Manual Notes and Logs

Sales reps can also manually add notes to any lead:
- **Call Note:** What was discussed during a phone call.
- **Meeting Note:** Key takeaways from a demo or meeting.
- **Email Note:** Summary of an email conversation.
- **General Note:** Internal reminders about the customer.

Each entry records who wrote it and the exact timestamp.
